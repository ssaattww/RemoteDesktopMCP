## 指摘事項

### R24-06-D1 — Medium: eight-shard adoption rule permits an unclear tradeoff

- Origin/location: R24-06 design, `doc/design/ci-test-runtime-reduction-design.md:93`; mirrored at `tasks/tasks-status.md:47`.
- Description/evidence: the acceptance text asks whether the median maximum Windows Test step falls below the five-shard observed 176 seconds “or” considers the full-workflow change, and says the issue remains open if neither metric improves or the overall maximum exceeds 180 seconds. It does not say whether an eight-shard candidate may be adopted when one metric improves but the other materially regresses (for example, a slightly shorter Test step with a slower full workflow due to added runner setup), nor give a decision rule for that tradeoff. The text correctly keeps the three-minute goal separate, but adoption and goal completion remain conflated in this paragraph.
- Impact: a reviewer/implementer could make opposite adoption decisions from the same successful data, and a small change in one metric could justify adoption despite a meaningful regression in the other. That weakens the experiment's decision value even though the measurements themselves are properly separated.
- Required action: define the adoption decision independently from Issue #24/180-second completion. State whether adoption requires no regression in the other metric, a named guardrail/threshold, or is exploratory with both outcomes reported and a later decision. Keep the per-run, median, maximum, and 180-second target rules intact; mirror the decision in R24-06 task acceptance.
